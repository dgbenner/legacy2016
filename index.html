About Dan

At The Creative Circus, I took three teams classes a quarter for two years. Each week, I presented at least 10 concepts per class with a creative partner — roughly 2,400 campaign ideas over two years. I like to think that's more campaigns than most creatives produce in a career. I won't claim they were all great, but I noticed they got better the further I went. Solid thinking is the floor. Great is something else entirely — and it's inspired.
